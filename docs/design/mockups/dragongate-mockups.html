Revised — one flow, research-informed
🔎 What changed from round 1: compared against Submittable, OpenWater, WizeHive, SlideRoom, and CaFÉ (the arts-application-specific competitor) instead of generic form builders. All five use the same shape: a checklist wizard wrapping a single-canvas builder with domain-specific field types, and dropdown field-mapping (Gravity Forms' field_map pattern) instead of freeform tags. This mockup drops the earlier 3-way split (Unify / Full builder / Wizard) in favor of one design combining the pieces that held up.

Considered & set aside

Your instinct to look for something simpler than a full custom rebuild was right — this is why the answer isn't a Google Workspace Add-on, at least not as the primary surface.

Why configuration stays in WordPress

WP admin (this design)
  • Same surface as the rest of the plugin — no second login, no second product to learn
  • No distribution review — ships whenever you ship
  • Configures the actual public-facing WP form, not just the spreadsheet side of it
Google Workspace Add-on
  • OAuth + Marketplace review is typically 2–3+ weeks, sometimes 8+, and recurs on scope changes
  • A Sheets sidebar can't build or host the applicant-facing WP form — you'd still need this design underneath it
  • Adds a second Google-account auth prompt for a volunteer who's already logging into WordPress

Worth revisiting later as a lightweight companion — e.g. a "view submissions" sidebar inside the Sheet itself — but not as the thing that replaces this configuration flow.
