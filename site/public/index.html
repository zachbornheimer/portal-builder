The quiet part

No WordPress applications inbox. No copy of your applicants living on our servers.

When someone submits, their files land in your Google Drive and a row appears in your Google Sheet. A receipt goes to the applicant. That's the whole journey — no dashboard to babysit, no data we hold on your behalf.

For staff

Three steps to an open call

01

Build a form

Add the fields you need — nothing you don't. Text, files, tags, deadlines. It reads like a packet, not a survey.

02

Map Drive and Sheets

Point each field at a folder in your Google Drive and a column in your Sheet. Connect once; it stays connected.

03

Publish a URL

Share one link. Applicants open it, fill the packet, and submit. Your call is open.

One submit, three destinations

When an applicant presses the button, everything routes itself.

Files → your Drive

Uploads land in the folder you chose, named and sorted your way.

A row → your Sheet

Every answer becomes a column. Review, sort, and filter where you already work.

A receipt → the applicant

They get confirmation with their submission ID. No account required.
