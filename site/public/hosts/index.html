Hosts

Built for the people who run the call

Festivals, composer societies, and conservatories — often volunteer staff, always a real deadline, usually a Drive folder that already exists.

The shape is a jazz or concert-music festival’s call for scores: one opportunity, a prize committee, PDFs and recordings, a Sheet the artistic director already has open. That is the design center. It is not the only customer.

What hosting looks like

  • You stay on WordPress. DragonGate is a plugin, not a new venue.
  • Google OAuth is scoped to Drive and Sheets. No broad account grab.
  • Reviewers work in the Sheet and the Drive folder. They do not need a second login to see packets.
  • Anonymize is a portal option, not a lecture. Turn it on when judges should not see a name on page one of the score.

Who it is not for

If you need applicant accounts, a marketplace, or a checkout, this is the wrong door. DragonGate is intake for one opportunity at a time.
